I’m Dhruva Nagesh, a final-year Politics, Philosophy and Economics student at SOAS University of London. I’m also taking coursework at UCL (University College London) and working as a research assistant on sovereign debt.

My interests run through institutions, political economy, development, science and innovation, philosophy, cities, and infrastructure. Outside that work, there’s London, transport systems, history, music, art, opera, photography, maps, and various other rabbit holes.

This site gathers things I’ve put out into the world: a game, articles, and, over time, more projects and tools. My own writing lives on [Substack](https://substack.com/@dhruvanagesh).

## Institutions & political economy {#institutions}

I’m interested in institutions, sovereign debt, and development: how decisions get made, whose interests they serve, and how they shape what happens next. My [article for The SOAS Spirit](https://soasspirit.co.uk/how-the-ai-industry-speaks-the-language-of-public-interest/) looks at how the AI industry uses the language of public interest.

## Cities & the things between {#cities}

London, transport systems, infrastructure, and maps are recurring interests. I like paying attention to the systems that organize everyday life, and to the city around them.

## Looking & listening {#looking}

Music, art, opera, and photography are part of that attention too. I take photographs and have experimented with a digital walkthrough gallery; I’d like to share more of that here when it’s ready.

## Ideas & other detours {#ideas}

Philosophy, history, science, and making things often overlap for me. [Epistemic Clash](https://dhruvanagesh.itch.io/epistemic-clash) is a philosophy game I developed for an epistemology module at SOAS. It brings philosophical traditions into a playable card-game format.
